Privacy Policy

Effective 19 November 2026

This policy covers every mobile game published by LL Games on Google Play and the Apple App Store, including Planet Merge and any game we publish later ("the games"). LL Games is run by independent developers based in Georgia.

The short version
  • You don't make an account with us, and we don't ask for your name, email or location.
  • Your progress is saved on your own phone. We don't run servers that collect it.
  • The free games show ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device data to show and measure those ads, and in some regions it asks for your consent first.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play or the App Store. We never see your payment details.

1. Information we collect

LL Games does not collect, store or sell personal information on its own servers. We have no user accounts, no sign-up and no server-side database of players.

Stored only on your device

Each game saves things like your progress, scores, unlocked items, settings (sound, vibration) and whether you bought Remove Ads. This data stays on your phone. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the game.

3. Ads and your choices

  • Consent. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland or another region where it is required, the game shows Google's consent form before personalised ads are requested. You can change your choice any time in the game's Settings → Privacy options.
  • iPhone and iPad. iOS asks whether the game may track you across apps. If you say no, ads are still shown but are not personalised using your IDFA.
  • Android. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Remove Ads. Buying Remove Ads turns off banner and between-round ads. Optional rewarded ads (for example "watch an ad to continue") stay available only if you choose them.

4. Why data is processed

Data is processed to run the games, show and measure ads, prevent ad fraud, process purchases and show achievements and leaderboards. Where the law requires it (for example the GDPR), personalised ads rely on your consent, and the rest relies on the legitimate interest of providing a free, ad-supported game or on performing the purchase you asked for.

5. Children

The games are not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through one of our games, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. The only data shared is what the services listed in section 2 collect directly through the games.

7. Data retention and deletion

Game data stays on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the game. Data collected by Google or Apple is kept according to their policies; you can manage or delete it through your Google or Apple account. Because we hold no personal data ourselves, we usually can't look up or delete anything on our side, but we're happy to help you reach the right provider.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, the Law of Georgia on Personal Data Protection or US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. To use these rights, contact us at the address below. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Security

All network traffic from the games goes to Google or Apple over encrypted connections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we keep the games' data handling to a minimum.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when a game adds a new service. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
